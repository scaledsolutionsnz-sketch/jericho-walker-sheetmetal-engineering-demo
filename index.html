What we make

Metalwork, cut and machined right

Sheetmetal fabrication and precision machining for builders, fabricators and businesses across Canterbury.

Custom Sheetmetal Fabrication

Brackets, enclosures, flashings, panels and guards — cut and folded from your drawings in steel, stainless or aluminium.

Precision Machining

Turning and milling for one-off components and short runs, held to tight tolerances so the part fits first time.

Stainless Steel Work

Food-grade benches, hygienic fittings and corrosion-resistant fabrication for kitchens, dairy and processing.

Cutting & Folding

Plasma and laser cutting plus press-brake folding for clean edges and repeatable parts, batch after batch.

Welding & Assembly

TIG and MIG welding, frames and finished assemblies — built square and ready to bolt straight in.

Repairs & One-Offs

Prototypes, replacement parts and repairs when off-the-shelf won't do or the original is no longer made.

About the workshop

Metalwork done properly, here in Christchurch

Metal World is a Christchurch workshop turning out custom sheetmetal and machined parts — the kind of work that has to fit a drawing, not just look about right.

Bring us a sketch, a CAD file or a worn-out part to copy. We'll quote it clearly, make it to spec and tell you straight if there's a better way to do the job.

  • Locally owned and operated in Christchurch
  • Work to your drawings and tolerances
  • Fixed quotes agreed before we start
  • Steel, stainless and aluminium under one roof
