Privacy Policy

How Metal World handles the information you share with us. This is placeholder content for the demo and should be reviewed before going live.

1. What we collect

When you contact us through this website we collect the details you provide — typically your name, email, phone number, suburb and the details of the job you're enquiring about. We only collect what we need to quote and complete your work.

2. How we use it

Your information is used to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, carry out work and keep in touch about your job. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

3. Form submissions

Enquiry and feedback forms on this site are delivered to us by email via a third-party form service (FormSubmit). Information submitted through those forms is transmitted to our business email address.

4. Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to limit access to those who need it to do their job. We retain enquiry information only for as long as it is needed.

5. Sharing

We don't share your personal information with third parties except where needed to complete your work (for example, a material supplier) or where required by law.

6. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request corrections. Contact us using the details below.

7. Contact

For any privacy question, email machining@metalworld.co.nz or phone 03 365 0226.
